## Nail before hammer: every task opens with a Frame (founder standing rule 2026-11-17; nail-frame v1)

Source: Kai-Fu Lee, "AI Native": don't polish the hammer, name the nail. Before any task, subtask, agent, feature or experiment, write a five-line Frame and work against it:

- WHO: one specific person (a named role, never "users")
- CONTEXT: the moment and situation they are in when they need this
- JOB: what they are trying to get done, in their words
- BREAKTHROUGH: what becomes possible for them that was not before (would they raise a hand to buy it now, not a few-percent tune)
- FIT: how this piece coalesces with the rest of CWN toward that breakthrough

A blank or generic line means stop and frame it first. Every spawned agent's brief, report, PR and handoff opens with the Frame, and done includes checking the result against it. This applies on every surface (cloud, PC, Duet).
